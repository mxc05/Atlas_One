# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [Date]

This Privacy Policy explains how Controve ("**we**," "**us**," "**our**")
handles information in connection with Atlas One (the "**Platform**"). It's
written to comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023
(DPDP Act) and applicable Indian data protection law.

**The most important thing to know:** Atlas One is delivered as a
template/system that you duplicate into your own account. Once duplicated,
**we do not have access to, and do not store, the client records,
invoices, expenses, GST figures, or any other business data you enter into
your copy.** That data lives on the platform your account is on (e.g.,
Notion) and is governed by that platform's own privacy practices, which we
encourage you to review separately.

We collect only what's needed to sell you the template, support you, and

**Purchase and account information:** name, email address, and payment
details processed via [payment processor — e.g., Razorpay/Stripe] at

processor handles that under its own PCI-compliant systems.

**Support communications:** anything you send us via email or a support
form, if you contact us with a question or issue.

**Website usage data:** standard analytics on our marketing site (pages
viewed, referral source, device/browser type) — [specify actual analytics
tool once selected, e.g., Plausible/Google Analytics].

**What we explicitly do not collect:** the contents of your duplicated
Atlas One workspace — your clients, invoices, receipts, expenses, GST
figures, documents, or any other business data. We have no technical
access to it once you've duplicated the template into your own account,
because it never passes through our servers.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To process your purchase and deliver access to the template
- To send you important service communications (e.g., a template update,
  a change to these policies)
- With your consent, to send product updates or marketing communications
  — you can opt out at any time
- To understand aggregate usage of our marketing website, to improve it

## 3. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your name, email, and purchase information on the basis of
contractual necessity (to deliver what you purchased) and, for any
marketing communications, your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at
any time.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share the limited data we hold only with:

- **Our payment processor**, to complete your purchase
- **Service providers** who help us run our own website/support systems
  (e.g., email delivery, hosting for our marketing site) — [list actual
  sub-processors once selected]
- **Law enforcement or regulators**, only where legally required

We have no involvement in, and no visibility into, any sharing you choose
to do within your own duplicated workspace — for example, if you invite
your chartered accountant into your own Notion account. That sharing is
entirely between you and the people you invite, using that platform's own
sharing controls, and is your responsibility, not ours.

## 5. Your Business Data — Where It Actually Lives

Because we don't store it, most of the questions people ask a financial-
tool provider about their business data have a different answer for Atlas
One than they would for a typical SaaS product:

- **Security of your financial data:** governed by the security practices
  of the platform your account is on (e.g., Notion's own security and
  compliance posture), not by us. Review that platform's own security
  documentation.
- **Backups:** governed by that platform, not by us. We recommend you
  understand its backup/export options independently.
- **Deleting your data:** since it's in your own account, you control
  deletion directly — there's nothing for us to delete on our end beyond
  your purchase/account records.
- **A breach of your business data** would be a breach of the underlying
  platform (e.g., Notion), not of Controve's systems, since we never held
  that data. [Note for counsel: confirm this allocation of responsibility

- Correct inaccurate data
- Request erasure (subject to our own legal retention requirements, e.g.,
