## 1. What Atlas One Is — and Is Not

Atlas One is a pre-built business organization system for freelancers,
consultants, and solo-run agencies in India, delivered as a template/system
that you duplicate into **your own account** (your own Notion workspace, or
your own instance of the Atlas One app once available). Once duplicated,
Atlas One runs entirely within your account — **your business data is
never hosted, stored, or processed on Controve's own infrastructure.**

**Atlas One explicitly does not:**
- Store any of your client, invoice, expense, or financial data on our
  servers — see the Privacy Policy for what this means in practice
- File any tax return, GST return, or other statutory filing on your behalf
- Submit anything to any government portal
- Calculate your final tax liability or determine which tax scheme
  (presumptive or actual) you should legally use
- Provide legal, tax, or accounting advice
- Guarantee the accuracy of any GST threshold estimate or accounting 
  calculation.
- Share your data with your chartered accountant, or anyone else, on your
  behalf — any sharing you do (e.g., inviting your CA into your own
  workspace) happens entirely within your own account, under your own
  control, using the sharing tools of the platform your account lives on

Atlas One is a structure and set of formulas to help you organize numbers
so you and your chartered accountant can make informed decisions. **You are

regulatory compliance.** Nothing on the Platform constitutes professional
tax, legal, or financial advice, and no advisor-client relationship is
